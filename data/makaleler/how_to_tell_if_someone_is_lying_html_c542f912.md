## 📝 Makale Bölümleri ve Detaylı İçerik

### Giriş

[preferred_source_button]

Spotting a lie means watching for a shift away from how someone normally acts, not for any single “tell.” No cue proves dishonesty on its own. The same logic applies whether you suspect a lie at work, in arelationship, or in an interview.

### Key Takeaways

- Baseline first:Watch how someone acts when you already know they’re telling the truth, then look for shifts away from that baseline.

- No single tell:Avoiding eye contact, fidgeting, and other individual cues are weak signals. None proves a lie on its own.

- Polygraphs are unreliable:Lie detector tests measure stress, not lying itself, and produce a high rate of false positives.

- Cognitive load helps:Asking someone to recount events in reverse order makes it harder to sustain a lie and easier to detect.

- Trust your gut:Instinctive reactions can outperform conscious judgment, though even trained professionals rarely beat chance overall.

### Establishing a Baseline

An example would include watching how the person responds to basic questions with straightforward answers like “What is your name” and “Where are you from?”

Pay attention to their eyes, and watch where they go. Notice how their voice sounds and look at their body language.

Once you have established a baseline and want to spot a lie, look for shifts in behavior.
